Can't find Skills in Settings? Skip it. Open any new chat, attach the kit file to your message like you'd attach a photo, and type start setup. That works on every plan, and the assistant will help you save the kit properly once you're up and running.

Say the magic words

Open a new chat and type: start setup. The assistant takes it from there. It connects your QuickBooks and Google with you (two clicks each, through each tool's own official sign-in, so your passwords never go anywhere), asks about your rates and service area, writes those files for you, and then proves each workflow on your real jobs: your real invoices, a real voicemail, real job notes.

Phones, payroll, and field-service tools like Jobber are optional. The assistant offers to add them at your two week check-in, one at a time.
